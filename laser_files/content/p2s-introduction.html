xTool P2S: Introduction

The xTool P2S is a 55-watt CO₂ laser cutter and engraver. A CO₂ laser generates an intense beam of infrared light that the machine directs and focuses onto the material being processed.

MEDIA PLACEHOLDER: Photo of the xTool P2S in the makerspace.

When enough energy is concentrated onto a small area, the material heats rapidly and is vaporized, burned, melted, or otherwise altered. By controlling laser power and movement, the P2S can perform three primary operations:

  • Engraving removes or changes material at the surface.
  • Scoring traces a line without cutting all the way through.
  • Cutting removes enough material to pass completely through the workpiece.

The P2S is an enclosed laser system. The enclosure, ventilation system, sensors, and other safety features are important parts of the machine and must never be bypassed or disabled.

xTool P2S: Completing a Job on Flat Stock

This page walks through the complete procedure for a basic job using a flat sheet of approved material.

1. Inspect the Machine

Before beginning, inspect the inside of the P2S. Remove scraps left by previous users and make sure nothing is obstructing the work area.

2. Place the Material

Place your approved material flat on the slats. The material should be stable and should not rock, bend significantly, or move easily.

MEDIA PLACEHOLDER: Video showing proper placement of a sheet on the slats.

3. Close the Lid

Close the P2S lid before preparing to process the material.

4. Connect xTool Studio

Open your project, confirm the correct P2S is connected, and select Process on Slats.

5. Select the Material

Choose the appropriate material from the material list. Do not substitute a similar-looking material without knowing what it actually is.

6. Measure and Capture the Material

Use the appropriate P2S measurement tools to determine the workpiece position and thickness, then update the camera image of the processing area as needed.

7. Position the Design

Place the design over the desired location on the material. Make sure the entire design fits within the available stock and processing area.

8. Assign Operations

Check every object and make sure it has the correct operation: Engrave, Score, or Cut. If the project contains several operations, verify that none have accidentally been left out.

9. Preview

Click Process and inspect the final preview. Pay particular attention to the position of the design, the processing operations, and any unexpected paths.

10. Start Processing

Send the project to the P2S and follow the machine prompts to begin. Remain with the machine throughout the entire job.

11. Monitor the Job

Watch for sustained flames, material movement, excessive smoke, unusual noises, or unexpected machine motion. If anything appears unsafe, stop the job.

12. Remove the Finished Project

After the job has completely stopped, open the lid and remove the material. Recently processed areas may still be warm. Remove cut pieces and loose scraps so the machine is clean and ready for the next user.

Your job is not finished until the machine is clean and ready for the next person.

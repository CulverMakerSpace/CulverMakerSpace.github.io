xTool Studio: Basic Workflow

The following pages explore xTool Studio's individual tools and settings. Before looking at those features one at a time, it is useful to understand how they fit together. Most laser projects follow the same general workflow.

1. Create or Import the Design

Begin by creating a design in xTool Studio or importing a design created elsewhere. Precisely dimensioned mechanical parts will often begin in CAD software, while decorative artwork may be created in graphic-design software or directly in xTool Studio.

MEDIA PLACEHOLDER: Video showing a simple SVG or DXF being imported.

2. Place the Material

Place an approved material flat on the slats inside the P2S. Make sure the material is stable and will not move while the machine is operating.

3. Select the Processing Mode and Material

For normal flat stock, select Process on Slats. Then select the material that matches the stock you placed in the machine. Different materials require different processing settings.

4. Measure and Capture the Work Area

Use the P2S measurement and camera tools as appropriate so the software has an accurate representation of the material and working area.

5. Position the Design

Move the design over the portion of the material where you want it to be processed. The camera image is an important reference, but you should still verify that the entire design fits on the material.

6. Assign Operations

Select each object and assign the appropriate operation: Cut, Score, or Engrave. A project may contain several different operations.

7. Check the Settings

Confirm the material preset, processing type, and other settings. Beginning users should use established material presets whenever possible rather than experimenting with power and speed.

8. Preview the Job

Click Process and inspect the final preview. Make sure all intended objects appear, the correct operations are assigned, the design fits on the stock, and there are no unexpected duplicate lines.

9. Send the Job

Once the preview has been checked, the project is ready to be sent to the P2S. The complete machine procedure is covered later in this training.

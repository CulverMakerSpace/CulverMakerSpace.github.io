xTool Studio: Right Panel

The right-side panel contains many of the most important settings for preparing a laser job. This is where you make decisions that directly affect how the project will be manufactured.

MEDIA PLACEHOLDER: Screenshot of the right panel with Device, Processing Mode, Material, Process Type, Power, Speed, and Passes labeled.

Device

First, confirm that the xTool P2S is selected and connected.

Processing Mode

For normal flat material, use Process on Slats. Other modes are intended for special situations or accessories such as rotary engraving.

Material

Select the material that matches what you have placed in the machine. Material selection is extremely important because appropriate settings for plywood may be very different from those for acrylic, cardboard, or another material.

Do not choose a material only because it has a similar thickness. If you are unsure what a material is, do not process it.

Processing Type

After selecting an object, choose whether it will be Cut, Scored, or Engraved.

Power

Power controls how much laser energy is delivered to the material. Increasing power generally increases how much material is affected or removed.

Speed

Speed controls how quickly the laser moves. A slower speed exposes each location to the laser for a longer period of time and generally removes more material.

Power and speed work together. For beginning users, established material presets are usually a better choice than changing these values independently.

Passes

Some operations can be repeated multiple times. Additional passes allow the laser to travel over the same path again, but also increase processing time and heat. Do not add passes simply because a cut failed; an incorrect material, poor setup, or another issue may be the actual cause.

Process

The Process button takes you to the final processing preview. Always inspect this preview before starting the machine.
